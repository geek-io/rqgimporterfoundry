# Bundled dependencies

The module includes Mozilla PDF.js (`pdfjs-dist` 6.2.108, legacy build) in `lib/pdfjs/`, used to read PDFs inside Foundry. PDF.js is licensed under the Apache License 2.0; its license file ships at `lib/pdfjs/LICENSE`.

- PDF.js: https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js

No RuneQuest rulebook text, art or other Chaosium content is bundled. Everything the importer creates is read from PDFs you own, inside your own Foundry.
